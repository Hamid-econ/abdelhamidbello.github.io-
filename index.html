About me

I am a postdoctoral researcher in the Department of Economics at Université du Québec à Montréal (UQAM). My research lies at the intersection of health economics, labour economics, inequality, and applied microeconomics.

I use large-scale administrative and survey data to study how early-life health and socioeconomic conditions, environmental exposures, and public policies shape outcomes across the life course and across generations.

I received my PhD in Economics from Université Laval (Canada) in 2025. Previously, I was a postdoctoral fellow at the School of Public Health at Université de Montréal.

I am also affiliated with the Groupe de recherche sur l’inadaptation psychosociale chez l’enfant (GRIP).
